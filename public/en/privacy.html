Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

👤 Account

Signing in uses Firebase Authentication. We store the account id it gives us and the display name you choose. If you sign in with email, that email is held by Firebase Authentication. You can play offline against bots without an account at all.

🎮 Game data

Your username and scores are stored in Firebase Firestore so leaderboards can exist. Multiplayer matches are coordinated through Firebase Realtime Database and the room data is deleted when the match ends. A Daily Challenge result is written with exactly nine fields: your account id, your username, the score, your reaction time, whether you finished the run, how long it took, the scenario profile you were given, your starting hand size, and the time you submitted it — nothing else. If you are signed in, your coin balance, the card skins you own and the day of your last wheel spin are stored in Firestore under your account, readable only by you; coins sent by the game's administrator are recorded in an audit log. While you are signed in, each open tab also keeps a connection marker under online/ in Realtime Database: your account id, when the tab connected, and what it is doing (menu, bot match, Daily Challenge, Legends or multiplayer — one word, updated when the screen changes; during a bot, Daily Challenge or Legends match it also holds that match's card counts, whose turn it is and how many page errors occurred — never the cards themselves); the server deletes it when the tab disconnects, and only the administrator can list it. If an administrator suspends your account, the end date and the reason are stored under your account id, and you are shown both. When a multiplayer match ends, a record of it (the players' names and ids, the winner, card counts, how many disconnected and when it started) is kept for 30 days for the administrator. If you are signed in and the page hits an error, the last five error messages (text about the code, with no web addresses) are stored for the administrator; you can ask for them to be removed.

💾 Stored in your browser

Settings, house rules, slap statistics, your Daily Challenge result for the day and your daily wheel spin's tier progress are kept in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device unless you are signed in and a score is submitted. Clearing your browser data removes all of it. The id of the last announcement you closed is kept too, so it does not come back.

📢 Advertising

This game shows Google AdSense ads in two places only: on four reading screens in the game — Game Rules, About, Top Players and Slap IQ — and on the Game Rules and About pages of this website. There are none on the main menu, in the shop or on the account screen — never during a match, and never on a Daily Challenge run. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads, and out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. How Google uses information from sites that show its ads is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

⚙️ Your choices

You can play without an account, delete your local data by clearing browser storage, and reset your slap statistics from the Slap IQ panel. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent message, which asks whether ads may use cookies and personal data; that answer can be changed at any time with the “Privacy and cookie settings” button at the end of this page in the game. To have an account and its stored scores deleted, contact us below.
