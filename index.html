Game Rules

Objective

Collect all cards to win the game.

Gameplay

Players take turns placing cards on the center pile.

Challenge System

Face cards start a challenge and the next player must defend.

Slap Rules

Players can slap the pile when special patterns appear.

  • Doubles: Two identical cards in a row.
  • Tens: Two number cards adding up to 10.
  • Marriage: King and Queen next to each other.
  • Sandwich: Two identical cards with one card between them.
